Who we are

A compliance practice built on measurement, not promises.

MeasurementPros was founded more than a decade ago to address governance and management questions driven by complex data flows and emerging AI systems. The work has always been quantitative tracing claims to evidence, mapping controls to requirements, building programmes that hold up when scrutinised.

The firm

MeasurementPros is a governance, risk, and compliance consultancy specialising in AI governance, data management, and cybersecurity regulation. We work with organisations across the United States and Europe from financial institutions navigating DORA to technology companies preparing for the EU AI Act to US defense contractors managing CMMC obligations.

Our practice is built around a simple conviction: compliance is an operational discipline, not a document exercise. A policy that describes the right process but has no connection to what actually happens in production is not compliance it is exposure. We build programmes that are measurable, auditable, and designed to withstand scrutiny from regulators, certification bodies, and counterparties alike.

We operate from Austin, Texas and Bratislava, Slovakia. The transatlantic reach is deliberate the regulatory landscape that matters most to our clients spans both sides of the Atlantic, and we have practitioners who work in it every day.
