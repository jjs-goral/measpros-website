What IVO Means

California created a formal auditor framework.
Most firms haven't noticed yet.

SB 813 directs California's Government Operations Agency to establish a registry of Independent Verification Organizations firms that meet defined standards for independence, methodology, and technical competence, and are authorized to conduct official third-party audits of automated decision systems used by state agencies.

The intent is distinct from self-declared "AI auditing." IVO status is a formal designation process, not a marketing category. The framework envisions state agencies eventually being required to use an IVO for certain audits the same logic that requires licensed CPA firms for certain financial audits, rather than anyone who does accounting work.

The framework is still taking shape. The registry is being built. Organizations tracking it now building the methodology, establishing independence, documenting credentials are better positioned for when agencies start requiring it than those who begin after the mandate arrives.

Potential Benefits of the IVO Framework

What IVO designation could enable

State agency AI procurement audits

California agencies procuring AI systems for high-stakes decisions benefits, criminal justice, child welfare, licensing may require IVO-conducted audits before deployment. If that framework takes hold, IVO designation would be the entry point for that work.

Public sector bias and impact assessment

Automated systems used in government decisions carry heightened scrutiny because affected populations often have no alternative. An IVO framework would be designed for that context specifically not adapted from enterprise software assessments.

Vendor qualification for state contracts

AI vendors selling to California state agencies may eventually need an IVO audit to demonstrate their systems meet state requirements. A designated IVO would be positioned to provide that audit as a neutral third party with no stake in the vendor's outcome.

Why This Matters Now

California leads. Other states follow.

California has a track record of setting the regulatory floor that other states eventually adopt CCPA became the template for a dozen state privacy laws. The IVO framework is early, but the direction is clear: states that deploy AI in government decision-making will need a way to verify it independently.

Organizations that work with California state agencies or expect to are better positioned by tracking this framework early rather than waiting for a requirement to land. The groundwork for IVO qualification takes time to establish. We're building it now.
